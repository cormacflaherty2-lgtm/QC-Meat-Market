About QC Meat Market

A San Marcos Institution

Quinta Caporales Meat Market has been serving the San Marcos community for decades. We started as a neighborhood carnicería and grew into a full-service market with fresh produce, a taqueria, bakery, and now a weekly public market that brings the whole community together.

Everything we sell is chosen with care and priced for real families. No corporate fluff — just quality food and honest service, every single day.
